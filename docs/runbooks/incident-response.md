# Incident Response Runbook

## Critical Incident Types
- Payment reconciliation mismatch
- Inventory oversell detected
- Unauthorized admin access
- Data breach suspected

## Procedure
1. Page on-call owner immediately
2. Isolate affected systems (disable payment endpoints)
3. Preserve logs and audit trails
4. Assess scope and impact
5. Notify relevant stakeholders
6. Execute fix per severity level
7. Document incident and lessons learned

## Escalation
- P1: Security/ops lead within 15 minutes
- P2: Engineering lead within 1 hour
- P3: Next business day
