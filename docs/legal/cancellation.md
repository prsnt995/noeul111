# Cancellation & Refund Policy (Draft - Korean Counsel Review Required)

## Cancellation Policy
- Orders can be canceled before payment confirmation
- After payment confirmation, cancellation processed within 3 business days
- Digital products not eligible for cancellation after delivery

## Refund Policy
- Full refund for canceled orders
- Partial refund for defective products
- Refund to original payment method within 5 business days

## Consumer Protection
- 7-day withdrawal right under Korean E-commerce Act
- Defective product replacement within 30 days
- Refund processing time: 3-5 business days
